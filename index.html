03 / HOW WE WORK

Make it concrete.
Then make it dependable.

  1. Frame the problem

    Clarify the users, data, operational constraints and what success would actually look like.

  2. Build a useful slice

    Connect a narrow, working flow. Inspect behavior with representative data and explicit failure paths.

  3. Verify and hand over

    Test the system, document its limits and leave a repeatable path for deployment and iteration.
