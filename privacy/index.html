PRIVACY / PLAIN LANGUAGE

Your data,
kept deliberate.

Language preference

Your language selection is saved in localStorage on this device. Shared links always keep their own language. Clearing browser site data removes this preference.

Interactive lab

The simulator runs locally. Requests and events stay in page memory and disappear when you reload. Do not enter personal or confidential information. No AI provider receives the lab text.

Project inquiries

The contact links open your email application. This website does not send or store your contact details on a Santensor server. When you choose to send, your email provider processes the message and the confirmed recipient receives it. Use the same contact to request deletion of an inquiry.

Hosting and assets

Fonts and assets are served with this site. There are no advertising cookies or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process standard connection logs.

Private trainer

The separate trainer requires login and has its own consent controls. Voice is processed by its AI provider; the app does not store audio recordings. Interview content is saved only with consent.
